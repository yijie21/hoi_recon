# 4DGS-first: dynamic Gaussian reconstruction as a temporal denoiser, then extract MANO + rigid-object SE(3) from persistent Gaussian trajectories

**Branch ID:** `b1-gs-scaffold-then-fit`
**Overall score:** 4.5
**Verdict:** maybe (reconstruct-then-parse cell unoccupied, but the advertised depth-triangulation mechanism is unsound for a dynamic object and GraG/CHOIR crowd the claim)

## Digest (card summary)

**TL;DR:** Reconstruct the whole clip as smooth persistent Gaussians first, then read hand and object trajectories out of them — betting jointly-optimized evidence beats filtering per-frame guesses.

**Why:**
- Per-frame 3D evidence is wrong: HaWoR wiggle 7.9x; filters leave 1.59x depth swing
- Bet: band-limited Gaussian evidence makes jitter unrepresentable; templates fitted afterwards inherit smoothness
- MoSca-class 4DGS matured 2024-25; multi-hour per-clip budgets already accepted (do-as-i-do: 3h)

**Pipeline:**
1. **Per-clip priors** — Gather camera poses, SAM2 masks, CoTracker3 tracks, affine-aligned DA3 metric depth, HaWoR seed. — `RGB video -> masks, 2D tracks, depth, poses, MANO seed`; reuses do-as-i-do stages (SAM2, DA3, HaWoR); HOI4D camera poses
2. **Band-limited 4DGS scaffold** — MoSca-style persistent Gaussians skinned to SE(3) nodes on cubic B-splines (4-8 Hz cap). — `priors -> smooth per-frame Gaussian trajectories (hand + object groups)`; reuses MoSca / Shape-of-Motion codebase
3. **Object Procrustes parse** — Closed-form weighted Umeyama per frame on persistent object Gaussians; IRLS/Huber rejects mask bleed. — `object-Gaussian trajectories -> SE(3) trajectory + TSDF-fused mesh`; reuses SAM-3D mesh for optional CAD-like registration
4. **MANO trajectory IK** — Bind hand Gaussians to canonical MANO once; per-frame Levenberg-Marquardt, no smoothness term. — `hand-Gaussian trajectories + seed -> theta(t), root SE(3)(t)`; reuses HaWoR canonical seed; 0.19m hand-size prior from render_and_compare
5. **Contact polish** — Detect contacts from motion field; refine with attraction, non-penetration, coupled hand-object updates. — `hand + object trajectories -> contact-consistent 4D HOI`
6. **Oracle-filter benchmark** — Score accuracy plus jitter against four baselines under oracle-tuned filters on HOI4D GT. — `reconstructions -> MPJPE/ADD-S + wiggle/flicker/swing tables`; reuses user's compare/hoi4d harness + 4 baseline pipelines

**What's new:** GraG (arXiv 2604.12929) tracks frozen object Gaussians with smoothness penalties; here jitter is unrepresentable in a spline-band-limited Gaussian field, and MANO plus object SE(3) are parsed post-hoc.

**Top risks:**
- ⚠ Monocular photometrics can't fix dynamic-object absolute depth: consistency without correctness
- ⚠ Band-limited template-space bundle adjustment (no Gaussians) may tie the whole pipeline
- ⚠ GraG/CHOIR/THO crowd temporal-coherence claims; delta may read as MoSca + Umeyama

**Kill test:** 2-3 weeks: run MoSca-class Stage 1 on 3-5 HOI4D clips. Go only if absolute object-depth error beats sequence-affine DA3 on GT depth AND band-limited template-space optimization (no Gaussians) loses; else pivot to benchmark paper.

**If it works:** Templates inherit smoothness by construction: match per-frame accuracy on HOI4D while beating oracle-filtered baselines on wiggle, flicker, and swing metrics.

## Branch definition

## Clarification

- **Problem Statement:** Monocular 4D hand-object-interaction reconstruction — recovering a metric MANO hand trajectory plus an unknown rigid object's shape and SE(3) trajectory from one RGB video — is today assembled from per-frame estimators that are only weakly coupled in time. On a GT-depth HOI4D harness, all four representative pipelines (render_and_compare, do-as-i-do/HaWoR, ForeHOI, HORT) fail temporally because each frame's 3D evidence is independently wrong: HaWoR hand depth traverses ~8x more z-path than the hand actually moves (wiggle ratio 7.9: 0.53 m path vs 0.067 m net), HORT's hand centroid teleports ~1.15 m in z between frames, and monocular object depth is biased -29% to -81%. Two controls show the failure is structural: swapping in a better metric-depth model (DA3) leaves the object's apparent-size swing unchanged (1.75x -> 1.76x), and output-space filtering (median(9)+moving-average(7)) suppresses only the high-frequency flicker (0.018 -> 0.005) while the low-frequency depth swing survives (1.75x -> 1.59x) — smoothing cannot correct biased evidence, only hide its jitter. The problem: within the already-accepted multi-hour offline per-clip budget (do-as-i-do spends ~3 h on a 5 s clip), make temporal coherence a property of the 3D evidence itself — estimated jointly across all frames — while matching per-frame accuracy (hand MPJPE, object size/depth/ADD) on the same harness.
- **Technical Core:** A two-stage reconstruct-then-parse pipeline in which a band-limited, template-free persistent-Gaussian motion field replaces per-frame estimators as the sole source of 3D evidence, and templates are extracted from it afterwards by robust registration.
  
  Stage 0 (priors, per clip): camera poses (HOI4D-provided; MASt3R-SLAM/MegaSAM in the wild); point-prompted SAM2 hand/object masks (text prompts already shown to lock onto wrong instances); CoTracker3/TAPIR long-range 2D tracks on the foreground; per-frame metric monocular depth (DA3-metric) aligned by a SINGLE per-sequence affine (scale, shift); HaWoR per-frame MANO only as a seed (median beta, one canonical-frame pose).
  
  Stage 1 (temporal denoiser): a MoSca-style persistent dynamic-Gaussian reconstruction of the foreground. Canonical Gaussians (unprojected from masked depth at keyframes) are skinned by dual-quaternion blending to a sparse SE(3) motion scaffold (~100-200 nodes hand group, ~20-50 object group, grouped by lifted masks — still no template). The critical design choice: node trajectories live in a low-rank temporal basis (cubic B-splines, knot spacing 0.25-0.4 s, i.e. a <=4-8 Hz band limit at 30 fps), so frame-rate jitter is UNREPRESENTABLE — smoothness is a property of the parameterization, not a penalty; simultaneously, joint photometric consistency (L1+D-SSIM) of the SAME persistent Gaussians across all frames triangulates absolute depth from ego-motion + in-hand object-rotation parallax, removing the low-frequency depth bias that filtering provably cannot (the residual 1.59x swing). Additional losses: mask rendering, sequence-affine depth prior, 2D-track reprojection (long-range correspondence through occlusion), ARAP/isometry on scaffold edges, and a soft intra-object rigidity term whose residual is kept as a regrasp/articulation diagnostic.
  
  Stage 2 (parse — templates never inside the GS loop): (a) Object SE(3) trajectory: because persistent Gaussians give explicit cross-time correspondence, per-frame pose is a CLOSED-FORM weighted Umeyama/Procrustes (scale fixed to 1) between canonical and frame-t object-Gaussian positions, robustified by IRLS/Huber against mask bleed and non-rigid stragglers — no ICP, no learned matcher, no pose network. Object shape: TSDF-fuse GS depth renders expressed in the recovered object frame (in-hand scanning for free), optionally registering a SAM-3D mesh once for a CAD-like output. (b) Hand: fit MANO once in the canonical frame (HaWoR seed), bind each hand Gaussian to its nearest MANO surface point (barycentric + LBS weights), then solve theta(t) and root SE(3)(t) per frame by Levenberg-Marquardt on Huber point-to-correspondence residuals with only a weak pose prior and NO temporal smoothness term — the headline ablation shows adding one changes nothing because the evidence is already band-limited, whereas per-frame baselines need filtering and still keep their low-frequency bias. Metric scale is resolved once per sequence via the depth affine plus a canonical hand-size prior on beta (~0.19 m — the same fix that recovered render_and_compare's 0.77x hand). (c) Contact polish: contacts detected from the stage-1 motion field itself (sustained proximity + near-zero relative tangential velocity between hand Gaussians and object surface), then a short joint refinement with contact attraction, MANO-vs-object-SDF non-penetration, and grasp-preserving coupled updates (hand and object moved by the same delta).
  
  Evaluation: HOI4D rigid categories with GT align_depth + GT object pose + MANO GT — accuracy (hand MPJPE/MRRPE, object CD/ADD-S, size/depth error) AND temporal metrics (wiggle ratio, acceleration error, apparent-size flicker and swing, contact stability, penetration), against the four pipelines raw and with ORACLE-TUNED filters (OneEuro/median+MA/spline/Kalman) — the decisive control — plus template-in-the-loop GS/implicit comparators (BIGS, HOLD) and DexYCB/HO3D for cross-paper comparability.
- **Novelty Claim:** First reconstruct-then-parse pipeline for monocular 4D HOI in which a template-free, band-limited persistent-Gaussian motion field is the sole 3D evidence and MANO + object SE(3) trajectories are extracted afterwards by registration against its time-correspondent trajectories (closed-form per-frame Procrustes for the object, skinning-bound trajectory IK for the hand) — making temporal coherence a property of the evidence rather than of output filtering (shown to fix only the high-frequency band: flicker 0.018->0.005 but swing stuck at 1.59x) or of template-in-the-loop rendering losses (BIGS, DyTact, HOLD, Interaction-Aware 4DGS), and contributing the first temporal-coherence benchmark for HOI on HOI4D GT depth with oracle-filtered per-frame baselines as the control.
- **Target Venue:** CVPR (2027, deadline ~Nov 2026) — a 3D-reconstruction systems + analysis paper with a strong empirical harness; no learned component is central (rules out ICLR) and no robot deployment (rules out CoRL); fallback ICCV/3DV.
- **Key Assumptions:**
  - Parallax sufficiency: egocentric camera motion plus in-hand object rotation give enough effective baseline for joint photometric consistency to anchor absolute depth better than any per-frame metric-depth prior; on low-parallax segments the method must degrade gracefully to sequence-affine-aligned depth-prior quality rather than diverge.
  - Monocular dynamic-GS optimization (MoSca / Shape-of-Motion class) converges on egocentric HOI clips despite hand texture-poverty, motion blur, and mutual hand-object occlusion, provided masks, long-range 2D tracks, and depth priors; scaffold persistence carries occluded Gaussians well enough that per-frame registration has usable support on both sides of every occlusion.
  - Smoothness transfer: because cross-time correspondence is persistent by construction (no per-frame re-matching), Procrustes/IK against band-limited trajectories yields band-limited template parameters — no correspondence flicker at contact boundaries; this is the load-bearing mechanism behind 'smooth by construction' and must hold empirically.
  - Band-limit validity: 0.25-0.4 s spline knots (<=4-8 Hz) sit above genuine HOI4D manipulation dynamics but below the jitter band (HaWoR wiggle at frame rate); if real motion has faster components the basis clips them (tunable, but a bias-variance knob that must be reported).
  - Object rigidity within the clip (scope: HOI4D rigid categories — kettle, bottle, mug, toy car); regrasps and articulated objects are detected via the non-rigid Procrustes residual but explicitly out of scope to reconstruct.
  - Camera poses are available or recoverable: HOI4D provides them; in-the-wild results assume egocentric SLAM (MASt3R-SLAM / MegaSAM) succeeds on handheld HOI clips.
  - Segmentation lift is reliable: point-prompted SAM2 masks (text prompts already shown to grab the wrong instance on HOI4D) group Gaussians into hand/object well enough that IRLS absorbs residual mask bleed.
  - Seeding is cheap: one decent HaWoR frame (median beta + canonical pose) suffices to initialize the MANO binding, and metric scale is resolvable once per sequence from the depth affine plus the ~0.19 m canonical hand-size prior that already fixed render_and_compare's 0.77x hand.
  - Compute budget: multi-hour offline per-clip optimization is acceptable — already the operating point (do-as-i-do ~3 h per 5 s clip on the same harness), with the GS stage estimated at 1-2 h/clip on a single modern GPU.

## Pressure test

- **Failure Modes:**
  - Core mechanism may be theoretically hollow: for a DYNAMIC foreground object, monocular photometric consistency cannot triangulate absolute depth — a rigid moving object has an irreducible size/depth/translation ambiguity under a moving monocular camera (scaling object size and its z-trajectory together re-explains all images). Ego-motion parallax anchors only the STATIC background; in-hand rotation parallax recovers object SHAPE up to scale, not distance. So Stage 1 will make apparent size CONSISTENT nearly by construction (frozen canonical Gaussians => the 1.75x swing metric collapses trivially) while the absolute depth trajectory stays anchored to the same biased DA3 prior (-29% to -81%) via the sequence affine. Result: temporal metrics improve, ADD/depth-error does not, and reviewers correctly read the headline 'removes the bias filtering cannot' as unsupported — consistency was achieved, correctness was not.
  - The killer ablation the proposal does not include may nullify the entire Gaussian stage: fit the SAME cubic-B-spline band-limited basis directly to template parameters (MANO theta/root + object SE(3)) with the same photometric/mask/track/depth losses — i.e., band-limited template-space bundle adjustment (essentially HOLD/BIGS with a spline basis, seeded exactly as proposed with HaWoR + SAM-3D). If that simpler baseline matches on jitter AND accuracy, the reconstruct-then-parse architecture is unnecessary machinery and the paper's central claim ('evidence-side band-limiting, templates outside the loop') reduces to a parameterization trick that works equally well template-in-the-loop. An AC will demand this ablation and it is genuinely likely to tie.
  - The band-limited-basis vs smoothness-penalty distinction (the delta against GraG) may be empirically indistinguishable: a well-tuned acceleration penalty realizes the same low-pass transfer function as spline knots. The required ablation (spline basis vs penalty variant, same losses) plausibly shows no significant difference, collapsing the main framing against the closest prior work to 'we chose a different regularizer.'
  - MoSca/Shape-of-Motion-class optimization is brittle on exactly this footage: texture-poor fast-moving hands, motion blur, fingers self-occluding, object >50% occluded by the grasp. CoTracker3 tracks break at blur/occlusion; 20-50 scaffold nodes on a mostly-hidden object are under-constrained; DQB skinning tears at the hand-object boundary. Expect a meaningful per-clip failure rate (divergence or mush) that forces either cherry-picking (reviewers punish) or honest success-rate reporting that undercuts the 'drop-in replacement for per-frame estimators' claim. MoSca also needs per-scene hyperparameter babysitting, contradicting 'method-agnostic.'
  - Smoothness-transfer breaks precisely where it matters: at contact/occlusion boundaries the registration SUPPORT SET churns (mask bleed flips boundary Gaussians between hand and object groups, occluded Gaussians drop in/out of IRLS inliers), reinjecting frame-rate jitter into Procrustes/IK outputs even though individual trajectories are band-limited. The headline 'no temporal smoothness term needed' ablation can fail exactly at contact events — the frames the HOI community cares about.
  - Hand accuracy may regress: MANO IK driven only by 3D Gaussian-correspondence residuals discards image-space keypoint evidence; a single canonical-frame binding (one HaWoR pose, median beta) propagates any canonical-frame error globally; and the 4-8 Hz band limit clips genuine fast finger articulation during grasp establishment/regrasp. Plausible outcome: wins on wiggle/acceleration, loses on MPJPE vs Dyn-HaMR/filtered HaWoR — read as a smoothness-accuracy tradeoff, not a win.
  - Crowding/scoop risk is acute: GraG (04/2026) already owns 'temporally stable template-light Gaussian HOI tracking' with acceleration metrics at 36 min/100 frames; CHOIR (05/2026) owns 'temporally consistent 4D HOI via multi-stage per-clip optimization'; Interaction-Aware 4DGS already does template-free 4DGS on HOI4D. Another 4-5 months of concurrent work land before the Nov 2026 deadline. The surviving delta (parse-after vs track-with-penalties + oracle-filter benchmark) is a framing-level contribution reviewers can dismiss as 'MoSca + Umeyama + MANO IK — a combination of known components.'
  - Benchmark contribution is weaker than claimed: THO and GraG already report acceleration/temporal metrics for HOI, so 'first temporal-coherence benchmark' is dead as stated; what remains is the oracle-filter control and the flicker-vs-swing frequency decomposition — a strong ANALYSIS section but not a standalone contribution that carries a CVPR paper, and it is built on HOI4D GT (align_depth holes at hand boundaries, known-noisy object pose annotations) which invites disputes about whether measured 'jitter' partly lives in the GT.
  - Systems integration burden vs timeline: SAM2 + CoTracker3 + DA3 + MoSca-variant + Procrustes/IRLS + MANO IK + TSDF fusion + contact refinement, then 4 per-frame baselines with oracle filter sweeps, plus HOLD (~10.5 h/clip) and BIGS reproduction, plus DexYCB/HO3D for comparability — at 1-2+ h/clip over the dozens of clips needed for a credible benchmark, this is multi-GPU-weeks of compute and months of brittle glue engineering with ~4.5 months to the deadline.
  - Scope narrowness: rigid HOI4D categories only, regrasps/articulation explicitly out of scope, single-hand implicitly assumed; GraG evaluates HO3Dv3+HOT3D and handles the same setting faster — reviewers will ask why the 3-hour offline pipeline is worth it when the fast concurrent method exists and the accuracy delta is unproven.
- **Unsound Assumptions:**
  - 'Parallax sufficiency' as stated is unsound, not merely risky: joint photometric consistency over a moving monocular camera constrains a dynamic rigid object's depth only up to the size/depth/motion ambiguity; the claimed mechanism for removing the low-frequency 1.59x residual swing (triangulation from ego-motion + in-hand rotation) does not exist for the dynamic foreground. The honest version — 'we enforce consistency and inherit absolute scale from the depth affine + hand-size prior' — is a much weaker claim and must replace the current one.
  - 'Smoothness transfer by construction' assumes a temporally stable registration support set; occlusion- and mask-bleed-driven inlier churn at contact boundaries violates it exactly where evaluation focuses. It is an empirical hope, not a construction.
  - 'Band-limit validity' assumes genuine HOI4D manipulation dynamics live below 4-8 Hz; contact transients and grasp-establishment finger motion plausibly do not, and the assumption is being used to justify removing the very evaluation frames where per-frame methods fail worst.
  - 'Seeding is cheap' (one canonical HaWoR frame + median beta) is fragile: canonical-frame selection is itself a failure point, and errors in that single binding propagate to every frame with no image-space correction path in the proposed IK.
  - 'The GS stage is a drop-in, method-agnostic replacement' contradicts the known per-scene fragility and tuning burden of MoSca-class optimization on adversarial footage (blur, texture-poor skin, heavy mutual occlusion).
  - Implicit assumption that matching per-frame accuracy while winning temporal metrics clears the CVPR bar in 2026: with GraG/CHOIR/THO all claiming temporal coherence, reviewers will likely require an accuracy win or a decisive mechanism ablation, not parity plus smoothness.
  - Implicit assumption that the Gaussian intermediate is necessary at all — untested against band-limited template-space joint optimization with identical losses and seeds (the most dangerous missing baseline in the plan).
- **Feasibility:**
  - **Data:** Good. HOI4D with GT align_depth, object pose, and MANO is in hand and the eval harness plus four baseline pipelines already run (compare/hoi4d exists in-repo); no new capture needed. Gaps: HO3D/DexYCB runs needed for cross-paper comparability (GraG reports on HO3Dv3/HOT3D), HOI4D GT noise (depth holes at hand boundaries, imperfect object poses) threatens the credibility of fine-grained jitter metrics, and rigid-category filtering shrinks the clip pool.
  - **Compute:** Marginal on the stated setup. 1-2 h/clip (optimistic for MoSca-class + parse + contact refinement) x ~50-100 clips x (method + ~6 ablations incl. spline-vs-penalty and template-space-BA) + oracle filter sweeps on 4 baselines + HOLD at ~10.5 h/clip is multi-GPU-weeks; doable with a small cluster or sustained vast.ai spend, painful on one GPU. The real cost is engineering time: 6+ brittle third-party components integrated in ~4.5 months to the Nov 2026 deadline.
  - **Evaluation:** Strongest asset and biggest trap. The oracle-tuned-filter control and flicker-vs-swing frequency decomposition are genuinely good experimental design and already partially executed. But the decisive tests cut both ways: (1) does Stage 1 actually reduce ABSOLUTE object depth error vs sequence-affine-aligned DA3, or only variance around the same bias — measurable on GT depth in week one; (2) does band-limited template-space optimization without Gaussians tie the full pipeline. If either goes the wrong way the paper's core claim dies, so evaluation is feasible but has high self-refutation risk. Per-clip success rates must be reported to preempt cherry-picking accusations.
