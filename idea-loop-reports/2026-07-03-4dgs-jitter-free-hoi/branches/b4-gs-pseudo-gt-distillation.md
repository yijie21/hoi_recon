# 4DGS as a pseudo-ground-truth data engine: distill slow per-clip GS-HOI reconstructions into a temporally-stable feed-forward HOI model

**Branch ID:** `b4-gs-pseudo-gt-distillation`
**Overall score:** 4.5
**Verdict:** maybe (highest novelty of the tied trio but premised on an unbuilt teacher and one decisive ablation; gate, do not commit the 10-30k GPU-h engine)

## Digest (card summary)

**TL;DR:** Bet: HOI jitter is a supervision artifact — run the slow 4DGS optimizer offline as a data engine, distilling its smooth trajectories into a real-time video model.

**Why:**
- Feed-forward HOI (HORT, ForeHOI) jitters per-frame; joint 4DGS optimization is smooth but ~3h/clip.
- Bet: jitter is a training-label artifact — smooth pseudo-GT plus derivative losses transfers stability.
- GS-HOI teachers just arrived (GHOST, Interaction-Aware 4DGS); EgoInfinity proves the window is closing.

**Pipeline:**
1. **Temporal-stability benchmark** — Extend one-clip HOI4D harness to ~20 clips with acceleration-error, jerk, penetration, contact-stability metrics. — `HOI4D clips + GT -> multi-clip temporal eval suite`; reuses compare/hoi4d GT-depth harness (currently one kettle clip)
2. **4DGS spline teacher** — Jointly optimize MANO-bound hand Gaussians and rigid object Gaussians on a C2 se(3) B-spline. — `RGB clip -> MANO seq + object mesh + se(3) spline + contacts`; reuses winning b1-b3 GS pipeline; WiLoR/HaWoR, SAM2, SAM-3D, MoGe stack
3. **Automatic QA gates** — Auto-curate labels via mask IoU, depth residuals, penetration checks, and held-out-frame render tests. — `raw pseudo-labels -> confidence-weighted corpus (fail -> 2D-only)`
4. **Corpus scale-out** — Run teacher over HOI4D (~4k clips) and Ego4D hand-object segments, embarrassingly parallel, one-time. — `unlabeled videos -> ~10k-clip 4D pseudo-GT corpus`; reuses HOI4D corpus already in hand
5. **Derivative-distilled video student** — Train windowed temporal transformer on poses plus analytic spline velocities/accelerations and hand-frame object pose. — `corpus + DexYCB/HOI4D GT -> real-time windowed HOI transformer`; reuses HORT/ForeHOI student architectures; HaMeR/DINOv2 encoders
6. **Stability-transfer evaluation** — Measure stability retention versus teacher at 100-1000x speedup, plus corpus-size scaling curves. — `student + benchmark -> acceleration-error and scaling-curve plots`

**What's new:** Unlike EgoInfinity's per-frame-pipeline labels for robot retargeting, distills jointly-optimized 4DGS trajectories — including analytic velocities/accelerations — into a feed-forward reconstructor, claiming temporal stability transfers through supervision.

**Top risks:**
- ⚠ Smoothed per-frame labels or SmoothNet post-processing may distill equally well, collapsing the contribution.
- ⚠ Teacher is unbuilt; without GT depth it may be smooth but systematically wrong.
- ⚠ EgoInfinity follow-up could train a reconstruction student first; 10-30k GPU-hours at risk.

**Kill test:** Distill on ~100 clips under three label conditions — joint-4DGS, smoothed per-frame (render_and_compare+spline), existing-GT+derivative-losses — plus HORT+SmoothNet baseline. Go only if 4DGS labels beat all on acceleration error at equal per-frame accuracy.

**If it works:** Real-time HOI video model matching per-clip optimizer smoothness at 100-1000x speedup, plus a released ~10k-clip 4D pseudo-GT corpus with scaling curves.

## Clarification

- **Problem Statement:** Monocular 4D hand-object-interaction reconstruction is split between two regimes that both fail a deployment need: (1) feed-forward predictors (HORT, ForeHOI) and weakly-coupled multi-stage pipelines (render_and_compare/CHOIR, do-as-i-do) run in seconds-to-minutes but are temporally unstable — per-frame independent estimation yields hand-relative object jitter, floating/penetration flicker, and depth/scale oscillation (on our HOI4D GT-depth harness: +33–43% object-size error and −29% to −81% object-depth error for the feed-forward methods, with visible frame-to-frame instability in all four tested pipelines); (2) jointly-optimized per-clip methods (HOLD-style implicit or 4DGS-based) are smooth and accurate by construction but cost hours per clip (~3h per 5s clip for the best pipeline in our harness), making them unusable online and unscalable as products. The unsolved problem: obtain the temporal stability and contact plausibility of joint multi-frame photometric optimization at feed-forward inference cost, for category-agnostic unknown objects, and show this transfer scales with unlabeled video rather than with human annotation. The core hypothesis is that feed-forward jitter is a supervision artifact — existing training labels (DexYCB/HOI4D/HO3D) and losses are per-frame, so no temporal signal exists to learn from — not an architectural limit; hence temporally-coherent pseudo-GT with derivative-level losses should transfer stability into a network that runs in real time.
- **Technical Core:** A three-stage data-engine + distillation recipe. (A) TEACHER (offline, hours/clip, embarrassingly parallel): a template-embedded compositional dynamic Gaussian-splatting optimizer — hand Gaussians bound to MANO mesh faces and driven by LBS from per-frame theta_t and shared beta; object Gaussians rigid, driven by a continuous SE(3) trajectory parameterized as a cumulative B-spline in se(3) (C2-smooth by construction, giving ANALYTIC velocities and accelerations); free background Gaussians absorb the rest of the scene. Jointly optimized over all T frames with: RGB photometric rendering loss, SAM2 hand/object mask losses, metric-depth alignment (MoGe, or GT depth where available as in the HOI4D harness), world-frame stabilization via HaWoR/SLAM so smoothness is imposed in the world frame not the jittering egocentric camera frame, and contact coupling (attraction between nearest hand/object Gaussians on mask-overlap frames + SDF penetration penalty). Initialized from the existing perception stack (WiLoR/HaWoR hand, SAM-3D object shape prior). Emits per clip: MANO parameter sequence, canonical object mesh, SE(3) spline, per-vertex contact probabilities, and per-frame per-part confidence scalars (rendered-mask IoU, photometric residual, depth residual). (B) AUTOMATIC QA GATES that make noisy pseudo-GT usable without human review: rendered-silhouette IoU and 2D-keypoint reprojection thresholds, depth-residual bounds, physics sanity (max penetration depth, contact persistence during grasp), and a held-out-frame render test (frames excluded from optimization must still be explained by the model). Passing clips enter the corpus with confidence weights; failing frames degrade to 2D-only supervision. (C) STUDENT: a windowed video HOI transformer — frozen-ish ViT per-frame encoder (HaMeR/DINOv2 features), temporal self-attention over 16–32-frame sliding windows, heads for the MANO theta-sequence, object shape tokens (point-cloud or SDF latent decoded once per clip), and per-frame object SE(3) + metric scale. The novelty-carrying objective distills DERIVATIVES, not just poses: (i) confidence-weighted per-frame MANO/vertex L1 and object-pose geodesic loss; (ii) velocity- and acceleration-matching of hand joints and object pose against the teacher spline's analytic derivatives (the stability-transfer term); (iii) a hand-frame relative-pose consistency loss — the object pose expressed in the hand coordinate frame must match the teacher, directly suppressing the hand-relative jitter/floating all four baselines exhibit; (iv) contact-map BCE from teacher contact labels; (v) mixed-batch training with existing GT datasets (DexYCB/HOI4D labels) to anchor metric accuracy and prevent teacher-bias collapse (e.g., the SAM-3D canonical-aspect-ratio bias that produced −21% kettle size in our harness). Corpus: HOI4D clips treated as unlabeled (~4k clips), Ego4D hand-object segments, and wild clips. Evaluation on the existing harness plus DexYCB/HO3D: per-frame MPJPE/Chamfer/F-score AND temporal metrics (SmoothNet-style acceleration error, object-pose jerk, contact-stability rate, penetration), with the two headline plots being (a) stability-retention: student acceleration error approaches teacher's at 100–1000x speedup while per-frame accuracy matches or beats its pretrained baseline, and (b) data-engine scaling: temporal metrics improve monotonically with pseudo-labeled corpus size.
- **Novelty Claim:** First method to convert slow jointly-optimized template-embedded 4D-Gaussian-splatting hand-object reconstructions into a scalable pseudo-ground-truth data engine — distilling not only poses but their analytic spline velocities/accelerations, hand-relative object trajectories, and contact states through automatic render/physics QA gates into a real-time feed-forward video model — demonstrating that temporal stability in HOI reconstruction is a transferable property of supervision rather than of architecture or test-time optimization.
- **Target Venue:** CVPR (primary — data-engine + reconstruction story with vision benchmarks, precedent: Depth Anything, WHAM; NeurIPS as alternate if framed as the distillation recipe, CoRL only if extended with a dexterous-retargeting downstream task)
- **Key Assumptions:**
  - Teacher existence and quality: a GS-based per-clip optimizer (winning b1-b3 variant, or GHOST / Interaction-Aware-4DGS class) matches the best current pipeline's per-frame accuracy on the HOI4D GT-depth harness while being substantially smoother — the engine cannot distill stability or accuracy it does not have.
  - QA-gate validity: automatic proxies (rendered-mask IoU, photometric/depth residuals, penetration/contact-persistence checks, held-out-frame renders) correlate strongly enough with true label error to curate the corpus without human review, and residual noise is zero-mean enough to be handled by confidence weighting.
  - Compute economics: roughly 1-3 GPU-hours per clip times O(10k) clips (10-30k GPU-hours, fully parallelizable, one-time) is affordable, and a corpus of that size is sufficient for stability transfer at 4D-HOI's label dimensionality (scaling analogy from Depth Anything / WHAM holds).
  - Jitter is supervision-limited, not ambiguity-limited: given temporally-coherent labels plus derivative losses, a 16-32-frame windowed transformer can emit smooth trajectories without test-time optimization, and window-boundary seams are solvable by overlap blending.
  - Metric-scale anchoring: the teacher's monocular depth/scale anchoring (MoGe, hand-size prior) is accurate enough that the student's metric heads do not inherit a systematic scale bias — otherwise the student must be trained scale-normalized with a separate scale head (harness already shows monocular methods at +33-43% size error, so this is the riskiest label channel).
  - Teacher-bias containment: mixing pseudo-GT with existing labeled datasets prevents the student from inheriting systematic teacher failure modes (segmentation locking onto wrong objects, SAM-3D canonical-shape aspect bias, spline over-smoothing of genuinely fast motions like regrasps).
  - World-frame reliability: HaWoR/SLAM camera stabilization is accurate enough on egocentric and wild footage that smoothness constraints are imposed in the world frame — otherwise camera shake gets baked into the labels as object motion.
  - Novelty window (verified July 2026): published GS-HOI works (GHOST, Interaction-Aware 4DGS, CHOIR) are per-clip reconstruction methods only; pseudo-4D annotation engines (NeoVerse, Any4D) are scene-level, not parametric hand-object; prior HOI pseudo-labeling (Liu et al. CVPR'21) filters per-frame predictions for known objects — no published work distills jointly-optimized 4D HOI reconstructions with derivative-level supervision into a feed-forward model, but this window may close within 1-2 conference cycles.

## Pressure test

- **Failure Modes:**
  - The necessity ablation is the whole paper and it can fail: if per-frame pseudo-labels (EgoInfinity/VITRA-style, or the team's own 2.5-min render_and_compare) plus a spline/SmoothNet fit — which also yields analytic derivatives — distill stability equally well, the contribution collapses to 'we added velocity/acceleration losses', an incremental loss recipe no top venue accepts. The proposal itself concedes this, and there is currently zero pilot evidence that jointly-optimized labels transfer stability better than smoothed per-frame labels.
  - A near-free trivial baseline may match the headline result: existing DexYCB/HO3D/HOI4D GT labels ARE temporally sequential, so derivative losses can be computed on real GT today with no data engine; and post-hoc output smoothing (SmoothNet/OneEuro on HORT outputs) crushes acceleration error at ~zero cost. The core hypothesis statement ('no temporal signal exists to learn from') is factually wrong as written — the missing thing is temporal LOSSES and wild-data scale, not temporal labels. If 'student + derivative losses on existing GT + inference smoothing' lands near the distilled student, the 10-30k-GPU-hour engine bought nothing measurable.
  - Teacher does not exist and its accuracy is unproven off-harness: the winning b1-b3 4DGS teacher is an unbuilt prerequisite (sequential two-paper dependency), and the only exact results in the harness required GT depth injection, which is unavailable on Ego4D/wild clips — precisely the corpus that justifies the engine. On monocular wild egocentric video (motion blur, hand occluding the object most of the time, textureless/specular objects, MoGe+HaWoR error stacking), a photometric 4DGS teacher may be smooth-but-systematically-wrong, and the student inherits smooth hallucination. Smoothness is easy; smooth AND correct in the world frame from monocular RGB is the open problem being assumed away.
  - Motivation numbers rest on n=1: the entire jitter/scale-error narrative (+33-43% size, -29 to -81% depth, all four pipelines unstable) comes from a single HOI4D kettle clip. Reviewers at CVPR will not accept a one-clip harness as evidence of the problem, let alone as the evaluation; a proper multi-clip multi-category temporal-stability benchmark must be built first, which is itself substantial unfunded work in this plan.
  - QA gates validate image space, not 3D: rendered-mask IoU, photometric residual, and held-out-frame renders are all satisfiable by geometrically wrong Gaussians (floaters, wrong depth, wrong aspect ratio) — GS is notorious for this, and monocular video's tiny baseline makes held-out-frame tests weak. The harness's own SAM-3D canonical-aspect bias (-21% kettle size) would pass every proposed gate. Confidence weighting handles variance, not bias; the biases are systematic, so the 'zero-mean residual noise' assumption fails exactly where it matters (scale/depth — the channels already flagged as riskiest).
  - Acceleration-error headline metric is gameable and the GT for it is shaky: any over-smoothed predictor wins acceleration error; without a paired responsiveness metric (accuracy during regrasps/fast motion, tracking lag), 'stability retention' is a rigged plot reviewers will attack. Meanwhile HOI4D object-pose GT is itself annotation-noisy, so measuring jerk against it actively rewards over-smoothing; only DexYCB/HO3D (lab, known objects — the distribution the engine was supposed to escape) give trustworthy derivatives.
  - Compute-times-yield economics: 1-3 GPU-h/clip x O(10k) clips assumes near-100% QA pass rate; realistic yield on wild egocentric footage (SLAM failures, wrong-object segmentation — already seen once in the harness, occlusion-dominated grasps) could be 20-40%, pushing effective cost toward 30-80k GPU-hours for a corpus large enough to draw a credible scaling curve. If the budget forces a 2-3k-clip corpus, the 'data-engine scaling' headline plot (the CVPR hook) degenerates to two or three points.
  - Crowdedness and window: EgoInfinity (Jun 2026) already owns 'web-scale automated 4D HOI data engine with QA', VITRA owns the pseudo-label scaling curve for hands, ForeHOI owns 'feed-forward replaces slow HOI optimization at 100x', and GHOST's 13x-faster GS teacher erodes the offline-only premise. The surviving sliver (derivative-level distillation from a jointly-optimized teacher, stability-transfer claim) is real but narrow, and a two-cycle build timeline (teacher paper first, then this) very plausibly loses the race to an EgoInfinity follow-up that simply trains a reconstruction student on their existing labels.
  - Scope: teacher pipeline + QA system + new temporal benchmark + video student + scaling study is 2-3 papers of engineering surface compressed into one; in 8 pages every component gets attacked (teacher vs GHOST/Interaction-Aware-4DGS, student vs EgoGrasp/ForeHOI, engine vs EgoInfinity), and the mixed-batch GT anchoring needed to fix metric scale simultaneously confounds attribution of the stability gains to the pseudo-GT.
- **Unsound Assumptions:**
  - 'Existing training labels have no temporal signal' — false as stated: DexYCB, HO3D, and HOI4D provide temporally-sequential GT on which derivative losses are computable today; the honest claim is only about loss design and wild-data diversity, which is a much weaker motivation for a 10-30k GPU-hour engine.
  - 'Jitter is supervision-limited, not ambiguity-limited' — monocular depth/scale oscillation is partly irreducible ambiguity; a smooth-labeled student can resolve it by emitting temporally-consistent but arbitrary depth (stable hallucination), so stability transfer conflates variance reduction with correctness and the evaluation as designed cannot distinguish them.
  - 'QA-gate proxies correlate with true 3D label error and residual noise is zero-mean' — image-space gates are blind to systematic 3D bias (the harness's own -21% SAM-3D aspect bias passes all gates), and the dominant teacher errors (scale, depth, canonical shape) are biases, which confidence weighting cannot average away.
  - 'Teacher matches best per-frame accuracy while being smoother' — unverified: the harness's accurate configurations required GT-depth injection unavailable in the wild, the 4DGS teacher is unbuilt, and the 3h/clip figure comes from a different method; assuming the b1-b3 winner exists and generalizes off HOI4D is assuming the hard paper already succeeded.
  - 'Depth Anything / WHAM scaling analogy holds' — dense per-pixel depth with strong 2D inductive biases scales very differently from a structured ~60-dim MANO+SE(3) output; O(10k) clips of correlated egocentric footage is orders of magnitude less diverse data than those precedents used, so monotonic scaling curves on temporal metrics are hoped-for, not supported.
  - 'HaWoR/SLAM world-frame stabilization is reliable on wild egocentric video' — on Ego4D-style footage SLAM drift and rolling shutter routinely fail; wherever it fails, camera shake is baked into pseudo-GT as object motion, i.e., the engine manufactures exactly the jitter it claims to remove, and no proposed QA gate detects it.
- **Feasibility:**
  - **Data:** MEDIUM. HOI4D (~4k clips) is in hand and already harnessed; Ego4D hand-object segments are accessible. But the usable-corpus size is gated by unknown teacher yield on wild footage (segmentation/SLAM/occlusion failures — the harness already hit a wrong-object segmentation failure on its single clip), and the evaluation side needs a new multi-clip temporal-stability benchmark that does not yet exist (current evidence base is one kettle clip).
  - **Compute:** MEDIUM-LOW for an academic group. Teacher labeling at 1-3 GPU-h/clip x 10k clips = 10-30k GPU-h before yield losses (realistically 2-3x more per usable clip), plus student video-transformer training, plus the b1-b3 teacher development itself. Embarrassingly parallel and one-time, and scopable down to ~2-3k clips — but a small corpus guts the scaling-curve headline. On rented GPUs this is a $15-50k line item; feasible only with deliberate scoping.
  - **Evaluation:** LOW-MEDIUM — the weakest leg. No established HOI temporal-stability benchmark exists; headline metrics (acceleration error, jerk, contact stability) are self-defined, gameable by over-smoothing, and must be measured against GT whose own derivatives are noisy (HOI4D) or in-lab (DexYCB/HO3D, defeating the wild-generalization claim). The decisive experiments — (a) jointly-optimized vs smoothed-per-frame pseudo-GT ablation, (b) derivative-losses-on-existing-GT baseline, (c) student-plus-output-smoothing baseline — are all cheap for competitors/reviewers to demand and any one of them can nullify the contribution.

Pursue at scale only if joint optimization wins that pilot decisively; otherwise drop and keep the teacher as the standalone contribution.
